George Brown College, recently re-named George Brown Polytechnic, was established in 1967 and is in downtown Toronto, Ontario.
 The college offers over 200 full- and part-time programs across various fields and combines both practical and theory to better prepare students for the workforce. 

George Brown Polytechnic is staffed with instructors that are highly experienced and genuinely are invested in student success. 
They offer guidance, encouragement, and ongoing support, making themselves available to help students overcome challenges and build confidence.
 This supportive approach helps students stay motivated and succeed throughout their program.

George Brown Polytechnic is worth the investment for students who are committed to succeeding in their chosen field. 
Through quality education, experienced instructors, and strong student support, the college provides the skills, confidence, 
and opportunities needed to build a successful future.
